Built around a simple idea: slow down and look out for each other.

I Drive 25 was created to bring greater attention to traffic safety in the places where everyday life happens—our neighborhoods and school zones.

Our approach is rooted in community awareness and collaboration. We want residents to have useful information, drivers to understand the importance of safe speeds, and communities to have a voice in identifying traffic-safety concerns.

Founded by a retired law-enforcement professional with 17 years of experience serving the public.

Safer neighborhoods. Safer school zones. Safer choices.
