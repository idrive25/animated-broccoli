Built around a simple idea: slow down and look out for each other.

I Drive 25 promotes a 25 mph speed limit in neighborhoods and school zones, where children, families, and neighbors share the road.

I Drive 25 is a California nonprofit public benefit corporation recognized by the IRS as tax-exempt under Section 501(c)(3). Our work is education, awareness, and partnership with the communities we serve.

Founded by a retired law-enforcement professional with 17 years of experience serving the public.David Pecoraro, Founder
